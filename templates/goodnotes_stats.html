        How these estimates work

        Current exported library, not lifetime writing. PDF ink records are counted directly; rounded vector paths in Goodnotes’ Quartz exports are estimates. Printed graphics can match, and erased strokes or rasterized handwriting cannot be recovered.

        Distance and widths use the PDF page scale. They are not measured physical pen travel or pressure. Colors and widths describe recognized paths. Repeated copies of a notebook count separately.

        Runs in spare capacity. OCR, indexing and web builds take priority. Original PDFs stay unchanged.
